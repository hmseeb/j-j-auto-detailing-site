About J&J

Passion for Perfection,
Pride in Every Detail

J&J Auto Detailing is a professional auto detailing business dedicated to bringing vehicles back to life with high-quality interior and exterior detailing services. We specialize in deep interior cleaning, steam cleaning, carpet and seat extraction, stain removal, exterior washes, paint enhancement, and mobile detailing services.

We work on cars, trucks, SUVs, campers, and fleet vehicles, providing attention to detail and results our customers can see immediately. Whether your vehicle needs a quick refresh or a full restoration detail, we take pride in delivering professional results using quality products and equipment. Located in the heart of Thief River Falls, we proudly serve the surrounding communities.
